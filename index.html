Research profile

I am a geographer working at the intersection of GIScience, remote sensing, spatial statistics, and GeoAI. My research examines how environmental change, energy systems, and digital infrastructure reshape human exposure, public health, spatial inequality, and regional development, with a particular focus on East Asia.
